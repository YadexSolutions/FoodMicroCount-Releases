# FoodMicro Count Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FoodMicro Count aims to provide a welcoming, respectful and scientifically constructive environment for laboratory users, students, researchers and software contributors, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected conduct

- Be courteous and patient. Offer criticism of methods, code or ideas without attacking people.
- Make scientific claims proportionate to the evidence, and clearly distinguish evaluation data from validation results.
- Respect intellectual property, institutional rules and confidentiality.
- Share only images, datasets and laboratory records that you have permission to publish.
- Provide reproducible information when discussing bugs or counting disagreements.
- Recognize that users may have different equipment, laboratory resources and levels of technical experience.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, publishing someone else's private information, repeated disruptive behaviour and deliberate misrepresentation of software accuracy or scientific results are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close or lock discussions, reject pull requests, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed. Decisions should be applied as fairly and consistently as reasonably possible.

## Reporting a concern

For routine moderation, flag the relevant GitHub content using GitHub's reporting features. For **confidential** conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately using a professional contact method published on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/YadexSolutions). Do not publish sensitive evidence, personal information or allegations in a public issue or discussion. If no private contact method is available, you may first request one publicly **without describing the sensitive incident**.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

This is a project-specific community conduct policy for FoodMicro Count.
